HOW TO PLAY

Master the paddle, control the rally and beat the CPU before it adapts.

01

Move

Use your mouse over the arena, or hold W/S and the Up/Down arrow keys.

02

Shape the bounce

Hit higher or lower on the paddle to send the ball into a different angle.

03

Build a rally

Each clean paddle contact speeds the ball up slightly. Stay ready as the pace increases.

04

Finish the match

First player to reach 7 points wins. Use Pause, Reset or Play Again whenever you need.

TIP

Keeping the paddle near the ball's center gives you more room to react to sharp angles.

SPACE

Use Space for a fast pause / resume without touching the buttons.

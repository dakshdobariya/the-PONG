HOW TO PLAY

Keep the ball in play and beat the computer-controlled paddle.

01

Move

Move the left paddle with your mouse over the arena or use the Up/Down arrow keys.

02

Return

Hit the ball with your paddle. The bounce angle changes depending on where the ball hits.

03

Score

When the ball passes a paddle, the other side gets a point and the next serve starts automatically.

04

Pause

Click Pause or press Space to pause and resume the match. Reset Scores starts the score from zero.
